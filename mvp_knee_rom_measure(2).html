How it works. Patient lies supine. The camera identifies hip, knee, and ankle landmarks — equivalent to a goniometer placed on the greater trochanter, lateral femoral epicondyle, and lateral malleolus. 0° = full extension (leg flat on bed). Positive values are flexion; negative values are hyperextension.

For clinical use, validate against a manual goniometer. Camera-based ROM has typical ±3–5° agreement with goniometry under good conditions.
